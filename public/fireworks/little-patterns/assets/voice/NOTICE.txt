Little Patterns bundled voice

Voice: Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (Google), designed voice voice_l1goo4gqobvj,
"Nook": a kind grandfather from Northampton, England. The voice was designed
from a written description; it is not a clone or recording of any real
person, child or television character. No endorsement is implied.

Every clip in this folder uses that voice, including the 26 alphabet
(phonics) sounds. The previous Piper "Jenny (Dioco)" voice is no longer used.

The clips were generated once, on a build computer, with
tools/nook-voice-gemini.py, then trimmed and volume-balanced. The game never
calls a speech service: only these WAV clips and a text-to-clip index are
distributed. The audio carries Google's inaudible SynthID watermark, which
identifies it as AI-generated.

The project supplied the original sentences and word bank. Words outside the
built-in script are spoken by the device's own voice.
Rebuild instructions: docs/nook-voice-gemini.md.
