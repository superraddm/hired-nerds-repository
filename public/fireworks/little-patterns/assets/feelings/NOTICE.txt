Nook's Garden: Feelings illustrations

The four human characters, expression drawings, seated poses and everyday story scenes in
feelings-art.js are original SVG artwork authored for this project in September
2026. They extend the project's original Feelings design sheet. No third-party
characters, photographs, commercial emotion cards, stock art or emoji are used.

These original illustrations may be used, modified and redistributed under the
same terms as the game's LICENSE.txt. They are examples of expressions and poses,
not diagnostic pictures or a universal dictionary of a person's internal state.

The existing Nook mascot is reused from this project's assets/nook.png. Existing
Mulberry symbols elsewhere retain their separate assets/symbols/NOTICE.txt.
