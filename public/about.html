About

I find the manual work in a business and replace it with systems people actually use.

I build and run the systems a business depends on: CRM, customer data, email infrastructure, the integrations between platforms and, over the last two years, AI-driven automation.

At MTDCNC, a B2B media business serving the manufacturing technology sector, I have owned the CRM and marketing systems end to end since 2016. My job title says Digital Marketing Manager. The work is systems: a CRM of more than 20,000 contacts, email infrastructure that has carried around a million messages a month, and the integrations that connect them to the task board, the website and the video team.

Since 2025 I have built a set of agent pipelines in Claude Code and Python. They read a shared inbox and turn it into a task list, run tools from a task board, publish a magazine to the website and drive a video-edit pipeline. Every one runs behind a spend cap and a kill switch, and nothing leaves the building without a person releasing it.

Before that I ran my own consultancy migrating CRMs for UK insurance brokers, and earlier still I replaced spreadsheet processes with Access and VBA systems at a college and a telemarketing firm. The pattern has been the same for fifteen years: learn how the work is really done, model the data, build the simplest thing that holds, document it, and train the people who use it.
