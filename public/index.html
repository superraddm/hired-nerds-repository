What I do

Four things, usually on the same project

Automation and AI

Agent pipelines in Claude Code and Python. OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini APIs. Retrieval over company documents. Zapier, Apps Script and Monday.com automations where they are the right tool.

Integration

REST APIs, webhooks and OAuth 2.0 between CRMs, task boards, WordPress, YouTube, Dropbox and email platforms. Small Python, Node and Cloudflare Workers services that glue them together.

CRM and data

GoHighLevel, FiveCRM and CallPro, with working knowledge of HubSpot. SQL and MySQL. Migrations, deduplication, fuzzy matching and reporting straight from production databases.

Email infrastructure

Around a million emails a month at peak. SPF, DKIM, DMARC, reputation and suppression. More than 3,000 hand-coded campaigns and the lint rules every build has to pass.

How I work

Automation people can trust with real work

Capped and killable

Every pipeline that spends money runs behind a ledger, a canary run and a kill switch. It cannot run away with a budget.

A person releases it

The tools prepare the email, the post or the report. Nothing reaches a customer or goes public until a named person lets it go.

Written down and handed over

Each system has a handover document and an operations plan, and I train the people who run it. It should not depend on me being in the room.
