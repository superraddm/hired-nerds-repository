Little Patterns round 4: the build brief

The frozen brief for building the Nook frame, Shapes, the Nook-centred Feelings game and the faster speech. It folds in both Opus passes, all three Fable passes, Codex's pass and your eight answers. The full text is docs/little-patterns-round-4/BUILD-BRIEF.md.

Design frozen. Nothing built yet. Builder reports with images at each step.

Your eight answers, as recorded

  1. Parent locks the device, not the gameThe game removes its own in-page exits and stops there: no browser trapping, no service worker. A manifest lets it launch full screen from the home screen if you choose.
  2. No feelings colour schemeThe ten feeling cards are one neutral colour. A school scheme can be a Grown-ups option later.
  3. Letter keys in ABC orderNarrow phones get seven wide columns; landscape keeps ten by three. Keys are at least 44 px.
  4. This brief is the build briefOne file, every accepted item by number from Opus, Codex and Fable, with the overrides. The builder works from it.
  5. Nook: Codex's final sheet is signed offThe original Nook, traced into the rig, with the sheet's expressions. The builder's first report shows the traced stills and the ten face crops as images.
  6. Whole-sentence clips after you hear the samplesThe listening page carries the joined lines next to two or three whole-sentence recordings. Nothing more is rendered until you decide.
  7. Local onlyServed and tested on the home Wi-Fi. No kpopboom preview, no production, no push, until you say so.
  8. CommittedThe round-4 documents, mockups, screenshots and Codex's sheets are in git.

Nook: the original, with new feelings

You chose the original Nook over the vector redraw. Codex's final sheet, which you signed off, is the expression reference; the production geometry is traced from the original picture. The builder shows the traced stills and the ten face crops before wiring any motion.

Where the brief overrides an earlier pass

  • Item 60Two fixed action slots stay. A quiet Next before success is not adopted: every activity asks for an answer, and tapping through is the failure case.
  • Item 64Add shows the whole picture at once; arrival motion happens on Help. A wrong answer changes only the bubble: no head tilt, no posture change on Nook.
  • Item 54The original Nook's geometry replaces the vector body and face. The rig structure (position wrapper, breathing on the SVG, pose group, arm and leaf pivots, feet on the ground) stays.
  • Item 15No “diamond”. Level 1 shapes are regular, in varied orientation; level 2 adds pentagon and hexagon with irregular and rotated examples. A square is never offered with RECTANGLE.
  • Item 68 with J1In-page exits removed, manifest only, no service worker, Android back opens the shelf. Locking is yours.

  3. ShapesSides (tap each side, 22 px hit radius, tap-order numbering) and Name (three word answers), two levels.
  4. Feelings and Nook in playThe cancellable performance, the Me privacy contract, Which feeling?, Friends unchanged; Nook's calm responses in every game.
  5. VoiceFreeze the CSV of 50 new lines, render under the daily cap, trim and join offline, build the listening page. Wait for your answer on whole-sentence clips.
  6. ChecksBehaviour, privacy and viewport tests; the S24 and the iPad 5 in both orientations on the home Wi-Fi; a report with screenshots from the real devices.
