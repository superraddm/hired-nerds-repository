Proposal. Waiting for your approval.

What the research says

Little of it is specific to pre-readers, but it agrees: use conventions children already meet (home and back arrows), put a label with every icon, keep the steps few, make targets big and well apart. For "more inside" the one signal that needs no reading is the folder preview: the container shows small pictures of its contents. Every tablet home screen does it with app folders. A badge or a border is a symbol to be learned; a preview is the thing itself.

The shelf: grouped, with folder previews

The sub-menu: back arrow, activities, then levels the child can choose

In the game: a caption, not a button
