Builder helpers:
- `section(id, title, x0, x1, open, lanes)` writes the room and fills the closed layers with `cell` solids;
- `car(id, base, mount, home, drops, {sway, top, tops})` defines a carrier. `base` only sets which side its trolley trails on, because the art hangs every carrier from an overhead track. `tops` sets a lift height per destination.
- In hubs, decks that a carrier lifts through are gratings, which the ride check exempts.

### Rules changed in revision 2 (js/l3-world.js)

- **Carriers:** `home` is where his feet are when the gripper takes him at the waist, and the gripper sways ±`sway` on machine time.
  - Touching it (a circle of radius 0.6 at waist height) means a grab.
  - The destination is `drops[n % len]`, and `n` counts up with each grab.
  - States: grip 0.3 s, carry, release 0.3 s, back, rest 0.5 s, ready.
  - There is no letting go. Belt speed is cleared on pickup (this fixed a one-frame slide after set-down).
  - The loader rejects a destination inside another carrier's reach, or a carrier home or destination that stands on nothing.
- **Teach Cell:** unchanged rules. The page adds the start banner, and the screen adds the ghost preview (the path so far, and where it would crash; never whether it is right).

## Rules for anyone continuing

- **Levels 1 and 2 are not touched by Level 3 work.** Level 3 reuses the shared `world.js`, `puppet.js`, `art.js`, `machines.js`, `nightshift.js` and `audio.js` without editing them.
- Standing owner rules apply:
  - traffic lights, never numbers;
  - visible supports;
  - no dead ends;
  - no pointless switches;
  - the camera slows machines, never Mike;
  - no subtitles or new voice;
  - no real brands (in particular, no pale-grey arm with pale-blue joint caps);
  - debug switches are local-only;
  - salvage is never repair currency;
  - the "Hold E" helper stays at the pendant.
- The new rule for this level: **a mint-banded machine never hurts, and nothing hurts Mike while one holds him.** The mechanics suite checks that every ride is clear of scenery and of every non-boss hazard's sweep.
- Carriers grab on touch and cannot be let go of. Never place a carrier destination in another carrier's reach (the loader rejects it), or in a hazard's reach (the mechanics suite rejects it).
- Puppet note: arms held straight up are drawn behind his head (the arms are drawn before it). Poses that reach up must go up and out: the hang pose uses `armL_upper 0, armL_lower 20, armR_upper −100, armR_lower 0`.
- No deploying without Jof's word. When he says deploy, run `tools/deploy-kpopboom.sh` and verify afterwards. Note that this stages the whole mike-game folder, so Level 3 would go live, unlinked but reachable by its address, and locked off-local until Level 2 is cleared.
