# Mike the Mic: gameplay footage for the portfolio video

**Mike the Mic** is a three-level platformer that runs in a web browser, on iPads and desktops. Mike, the MTDCNC mascot, works his way through a factory where the machines have gone rogue. He never fights: he dodges, films with a slow-motion camera, and fixes things.

All the footage here is the real game, rendered frame by frame from actual play-throughs. Nothing is mocked up.

## What's in the folder

There are six clips, two from each level. Each clip comes in two versions:

- `mike-…mp4` has the game's status bars: health, lives, camera and score.
- `mike-…-clean.mp4` has no status bars, which leaves room for titles. In-game prompts and the slow-motion viewfinder (REC · 120 FPS) are still there, because they're part of the look.

The times below are approximate, in seconds into the clip.

**Specs:** each clip is 10.0 s, 1920×1080, 60 fps, H.264 at very high quality, Rec.709. There is **no audio**, because the game's sounds are generated live and can't be recorded this way. Please add music and sound effects in the edit.

## How you might use it

- **The three levels look different.** Level 1 is a dark teal night shift, Level 2 is underwater cyan, and Level 3 is bright steel blue. Played in order they show progression, and the bosses make a natural climax. A 20–30 s cut could be: the three level clips, quick boss cuts, then end on Level 3's ROUTE RESTORED.
- **The clips are 60 fps,** so they'll slow down smoothly to 50% for speed ramps.
- **Clean or with status bars:** use the clean versions under titles. The versions with status bars quickly tell viewers "this is a real, playable game".
- **For vertical (9:16) cuts,** Mike moves around the frame, so you'll need to keyframe the crop. Ask if you'd rather have vertical captures.

## Please keep

- Mike and the MTDCNC logo exactly as they appear. Don't redraw or recolour them.
- The machines in the game are deliberately unbranded. Please don't pair the footage with real machine-tool brands, logos or model names.
