# MTDCNC colours and type (measured 25 September 2026; confirm against the brand guide if one exists)

Measured from the supplied artwork, not guessed:

- **MTDCNC red: #EB0000** (the MACH poster background and logo boxes; the poster's dominant pixel values run
  #EA0000 to #ED0000). Deep shade for shadows and gradients: #CD0103.
- **Mike's foam and gloves in the posters: about #C0201B** (a darker, textured red than the flat brand red;
  it reads as the same colour under studio light). Render Mike in this, not in flat #EB0000.
- **Poster blue (Meet Mike background): #0F579B**, with the darker gradient edge toward #0A4A88. This is the
  natural base for the video's blueprint blue; the thumbnail navy MTDCNC uses on video overlays is #002C5A
  (from Y:\AMB-thumbnail-overlay.svg) and works as the darkest tone.
- **White: #FFFFFF.** Line work on blueprint blue is white at 100% for primary lines and about 55% for grids.
- **Black (Mike's stem and limbs, and the mono logo): #000000** with a satin highlight.

Logo: always the 2022 lock-up from `brand/source/CNC_Main Logo_2022.ai` (to be exported as
`brand/mtdcnc-logo.svg`): "MTD" knocked out of three red squares, italic "CNC" knocked out of a slanted red
plate. Over red backgrounds use the white-on-transparent version. Never redraw it.

Type: **Helvetica Neue LT Std** (files in `brand/fonts/`). Headlines Black or Black Condensed, body Roman or
Medium, small labels Light. The posters' headline face is a heavy geometric sans; Helvetica Neue Black Condensed
is the closest licensed match and is the family MTDCNC already uses.

URL written as **mtdcnc.com**.
