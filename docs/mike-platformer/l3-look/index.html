Cobot Mania

Mike the Mic, Level 3. Revision 4 review, built 2026-10-02 from the captures and test results beside it.

The design is in level-3-design.md; the state of the work is in L3-CONTINUATION.md. Revision 4 (2 Oct, after the owner found it too easy): the whole level is harder (eleven more hazards, faster machines with shorter warnings, two checkpoints and a lubricant taken out); in the Teach Cell, rounds 2 and 3 have two arms from two chutes, round 3 adds a blue crate for a blue pallet, and after every trip each arm sweeps one level of the cell, so no spot is safe from all three and a 2.2 s re-teach usually wants the camera; the end screen is tidied. Revision 3 boss (2 Oct): the Teach Cell is now a route puzzle in the room itself, and every level's HUD is two small pills. Revision 2, after the owner's first play: three times the length to the boss (eleven sections, each opening only some of the five layers); carriers that grab whoever touches them and send him on to the next of their destinations; redrawn cobots; and a Teach Cell that says what the job is and shows each move on its screen. It is not deployed, not committed, and not linked from Level 2.

Play it locally: node tools/serve-fireworks.cjs 8788, then localhost:8788/mike-game/level-3.html. Straight to the boss: ?room=boss. A section: ?room=5 (1 to 11). On the live site the page would open only for a campaign that has cleared Level 2.

The sections

Which layers each section opens, and where each carrier can send him. Closed layers are robot cells: walls, not pits, so every drop is between open layers and inside the view.

The level as built

Drawn from level-3.json. Mint bars: a carrier's reach (jump it or be taken); mint curves: its destinations, in order (solid first, dashed after). Yellow: hazard sweeps and rover tracks. Brown: crate drops. Dark: robot cells. Scroll sideways.
